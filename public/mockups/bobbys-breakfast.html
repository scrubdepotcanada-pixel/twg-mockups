Bobby's is the kind of place where the owners come check on your table and the baristas stay cheerful even during the Saturday rush. We took over the beloved space at 810 College and made it ours — coffee-focused, food that holds its own, and a clean space that feels like home.

Whether it's a quick caffeine run before work or a long catch-up with friends, pull up a chair. The vanilla almond latte pairs perfectly with The Lazy Apple.
